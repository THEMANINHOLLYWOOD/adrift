# adrift

thirty days adrift. a platform on the horizon. something under it. a tiny story game

## what you can do

- survive on the raft, days tick by, watch your needs
- paddle out to the islands, dive underwater
- reach the platform and find whats under it
- play with other people, everyone on the same sea (multiplayer over websockets in `api/`)
- full original score in `score/`

## run it

`npm i` then `vercel dev`, open the page. plain three.js, no build

assets and credits in `assets/CREDITS.md`
